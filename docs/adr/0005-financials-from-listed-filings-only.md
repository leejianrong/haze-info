# ADR-0005: Show company financials only from listed-parent filings, otherwise "undisclosed"

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Deciders: Jian

## Context

The brief asks for revenue and profit per company. Concession holders are often Indonesian subsidiaries (for example PT Mayangkara Tanaman Industri, owned by Sumitomo Forestry) or private groups with no public accounts. Estimates from third parties are hard to defend.

## Decision

Profile about 8-10 companies, prioritising those named by Greenpeace and in the 2026 top-ten burn lists. Revenue and net profit come from the listed parent's annual report, with the fiscal year and currency labelled. The page states that group-level figures are not the subsidiary's. Private or unlisted firms display "undisclosed", and that gap is stated on the page as a transparency finding.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                | Why not                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Third-party estimates                 | Not citable to a primary source; invites challenge   |
| Profile every company with burn scars | Too much research for the milestone; dilutes quality |

## Consequences

Some of the most relevant profiles will have blank financials. Fiscal years will not align across companies, so no cross-company ranking by profit is shown. Currency conversion is avoided: figures keep their reporting currency.
