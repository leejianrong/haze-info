# ADR-0007: Present government negligence as a sourced commitment-versus-outcome timeline

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Deciders: Jian

## Context

The brief wants a focus on the Indonesian government's negligence. Available reporting includes the cloud-seeding permit confusion between the Environment Ministry, the military and the Transportation Ministry, aerial firefighting grounded by haze for over a week, Prabowo's remark that "it really comes down to rain", and Trend Asia's finding that the top three forestry concessions by hotspots (275 hotspots) still have intact permits while others were frozen. "Negligence" is a legal and moral conclusion.

## Decision

The chapter is a dated list of `{date, commitment, outcome, sources[]}` entries. Pairs of promise and outcome carry the argument. The page may state counts (for example "N of the top ten concessions still hold permits") but contains no free-form editorial adjectives. Government responses and official statements are included alongside criticism.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                     | Why not                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opinionated "government failure" narrative | Easier to dismiss; weakens the credibility the rest of the site depends on |
| Omit the chapter                           | Contradicts the brief                                                      |

## Consequences

The reader draws the conclusion, which is more persuasive and safer, but less viral. Timeline entries need careful sourcing. Ministry statements change, so entries carry as-of dates.
