## Decision

We can use now:

- Singapore PSI, fetched live in the browser (Singapore Open Data Licence 1.0, CORS open, no key), with the attribution line above.
- NASA FIRMS hotspots, fetched by the Action with a MAP_KEY held as a repo secret and committed as a processed snapshot. The key must never appear in the repo or the bundle. Include the NASA acknowledgement.
- OpenFreeMap as the basemap. It needs no key and allows commercial use. Protomaps with a self-hosted regional extract is the backup. Do not use the OpenStreetMap tile servers.

We cannot use yet:

- Indonesian oil palm and pulpwood (HTI) concession polygons. GFW holds them view only, and the CC BY 4.0 grant explicitly excludes Indonesia. Committing a simplified copy would be redistribution without a licence, so assumption A4 fails for GFW/WRI.
- Nusantara Atlas burn scars and concessions, Auriga maps, SiPongi and government geoportal data, until the owner gives written permission. Their open reuse terms are unverified or restrictive.
- Logging concessions: no licence information found.

Fallback if polygons cannot be reused:

1. Ship V2 as a hotspots-only map (FIRMS points over the OpenFreeMap basemap). Company pages show burned hectares as sourced text with a link to Nusantara Atlas, not as a drawn layer.
2. Ask the owners for permission. TheTreeMap / Nusantara Atlas: admin@nusantara-atlas.org (listed on their privacy page) or the form at https://nusantara-atlas.org/contact-us/. Ask for written permission to display and cache simplified concession outlines and burn-scar summaries, with attribution. Global Nature Watch (WRI): use the contact route on https://globalnaturewatch.org (address not verified) and ask whether the Ministry data can be licensed for display. Auriga Nusantara: contact route via https://auriga.or.id (unverified).
3. Linking out to the owner's viewer is allowed in every case and needs no licence.

## Not verified

- Full Nusantara Atlas reuse terms, and the terms for MCD64A1, FIRMS (policy page returned HTTP 403) and GFW fire alerts.
- Any licence for Auriga, SiPongi and the Indonesian government geoportal. Current One Map access rules are known only from 2020 reports.
- GFW logging concessions metadata is empty.
